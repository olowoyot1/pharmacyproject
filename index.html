FAQ

Questions Before the Move?

Are we replacing ElectroClerk immediately?

No. The proposed migration is controlled: assess, configure, migrate, test, reconcile and then go live. ElectroClerk can be retained as a historical reference.

Is the POS part of Zoho Books?

No. The proposed OAATZ POS is a separate operational application designed to integrate with Zoho Books.

Does Zoho Books already have inventory?

Yes. Zoho Books includes inventory capabilities, with additional advanced inventory functionality available on higher plans. The OAATZ solution is intended to provide the business-specific POS and operational inventory workflow.

Can the system grow later?

Yes. Zoho Books provides API and customization capabilities, allowing additional applications and workflows to be connected as the business grows.

Can we see it before deciding?

Yes. The recommended next step is a practical demonstration of the complete POS → Inventory → Zoho Books workflow.
